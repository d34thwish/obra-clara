# Licencias de datos

Application licensing does not change source-data licensing. OECE catalog entries must be evaluated per dataset/distribution. The convocatoria and supplier catalog pages currently display “License Not Specified”; do not redistribute raw files until terms are confirmed. The OECE bidder-list catalog entry identifies Open Data Commons Attribution. Record URL, retrieval date, attribution and license snapshot per import. Demo fixtures are synthetic and CC0-1.0.
